# Voice guide: how I talk upstream

## Who I am in threads

I am a student developer learning open-source contribution workflows. I am reproducing issues so I can provide maintainers with clear, useful evidence. My comments should be specific about what I tested and should not claim anything I have not verified.

## Rules I write by

### Rule: Only claim what I verified

I do not say I reproduced or confirmed a bug until I have actually tested it and have evidence.

- Wrong: "I confirmed this bug and will post the results."
- Right: "I'll reproduce the reported behavior and post my steps, environment, and results."

### Rule: Be specific about the issue

I name the behavior I am investigating instead of posting a generic claim.

- Wrong: "I'll take this issue."
- Right: "I'll investigate the reported password verification behavior and post a reproduction report with my results."

### Rule: Report what actually happened

My repro comment must match my evidence, including when I cannot reproduce the issue.

- Wrong: "The bug is confirmed." 
- Right: "I reproduced the reported behavior with the environment and steps below."

### Rule: Do not promise a fix or deadline

I claim the investigation only. I do not promise that I will fix the issue or finish by a certain date.

- Wrong: "I'll fix this by tomorrow."
- Right: "I'll investigate this issue and post my reproduction results."

### Rule: Keep comments useful to maintainers

I include concrete environment, steps, and observed behavior instead of unnecessary filler.

- Wrong: "I tested this a bunch and it definitely seems broken."
- Right: "Using the environment and steps below, I observed the reported error."

## Things I never post

- A claim that I reproduced something before testing it.
- A promise that I will fix the issue.
- A deadline I cannot guarantee.
- Evidence or results that I did not personally observe.
- "Same as above" instead of my own reproduction evidence.
- Generic comments that do not identify what I tested.
